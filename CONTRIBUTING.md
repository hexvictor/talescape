# Contributing to Talescape

Contributions are welcome through pull requests and issues.

Talescape is source-available, not open source. Public access to the repository does not grant permission to redistribute, host, commercialize, or create derivative products from the code. See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## Contribution Terms

By submitting a pull request, issue patch, commit, suggestion, or other contribution, you agree to the terms in [CLA.md](CLA.md).

Only submit work that you have the legal right to contribute.

Do not submit code, assets, text, or other materials copied from third parties unless you have the right to contribute them under the terms in [CLA.md](CLA.md).

## Pull Requests

Pull requests should be focused, reviewable, and clearly described.

Use conventional commit-style language when possible, such as:

```text
feat(editor): add graph controls
fix(reader): correct scroll restoration
docs(license): clarify source-available terms
```

Maintainers may modify, reject, close, or remove contributions for any reason, including project direction, quality, licensing, security, maintainability, or product fit.

Accepted contributions may be edited before or after merging.

## Issues

Issues are welcome for bugs, improvement ideas, documentation gaps, and product feedback.

Submitting an issue does not guarantee that the work will be accepted, prioritized, or implemented.
